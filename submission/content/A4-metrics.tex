\section{The challenge statement's primary metrics}

\Cited{Section 4.1} of the challenge statement names ROC AUC, AUPRC, $F_1$, precision, recall and a
confusion matrix as the primary evaluation metrics. The body reports the first two, because
they are the ones the arguments there turn on. All six are below, for the
gradient-boosted baseline on the held-out temporal block at the default threshold, so that
nothing the statement asks for is left to be inferred.

Precision is \ClaimBaselineTestPrecision{} and recall \ClaimBaselineTestRecall{}, for an
$F_1$ of \ClaimBaselineTestFOne{} --- which is what a single threshold on a
\ClaimTestFraudRate\,\% positive rate produces. That trade-off is the reason for the
three-valued rule: the abstention band exists so that the decision does not have to be taken
at one point on this curve, and the certificate binds the error the band commits.

These are single-threshold summaries and none of them is a guarantee. A confusion matrix
describes one operating point on one block; it does not bound the error rate on the next
block, which is what the body's certificate does and what the temporal coverage result shows
a point estimate cannot be trusted to do.
