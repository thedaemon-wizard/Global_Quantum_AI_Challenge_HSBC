\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

\paragraph{Coverage holds under two control splits and fails under time.}
Table~\ref{tab:coverage} calibrates on $\Dcal$ and reports on $\Dtest$. The stratified and
card-disjoint arms sit inside the Beta-Binomial interval at every $\alpha$. The temporal arm
breaches at three of four levels.

The three-arm design exists to decompose degradation into a temporal component and a
sampling component. It does: the controls pass, so the breach is temporal.

\paragraph{But the breach is origin-dependent, and this narrows the claim.} Re-running at
five rolling calibration origins gives Table~\ref{tab:rolling}: the ratio ranges from
\ClaimRollingRatioMin{} to \ClaimRollingRatioMax. Two of five breach, and the earliest is
\emph{conservative} --- the deviation reverses direction. So the defensible statement is not
that temporal splits inflate the false-decline rate by a fixed factor; it is that the
deviation depends on where the calibration window falls, and a single origin cannot establish
its size. An earlier draft of this work quoted a single seed's maximum as a central estimate.
That was an overclaim, and correcting it removed our cleanest headline number.

\paragraph{What the certificate actually reaches.} \ClaimCertifiedConfigs{} of \ClaimRiskConfigs{} configurations certify, all
strictly inside the band --- the smallest margin below $\tauhi$ is \ClaimCertificateMargin. That property is
not automatic: an earlier version certified a larger set, every one of them
sitting exactly at the band edge, where the flagged set is empty by construction and the risk
is structurally zero. The certificate was vacuous and we did not notice until we asked where
the selected $\lambda$ sat.

The surviving configurations need $\alpha \ge 0.10$ and $\alpha_{\mathrm{FN}} = 0.45$.
Nothing certifies at the tightest band budget at all. The mechanism is sample size, not
method: at a 2\,\% traffic budget $\Dcal$ contributes only \ClaimBandLegitCalMin{} legitimate in-band rows
against \ClaimCalLegitCount{} overall, and the class-conditional degeneracy floor
$\lceil 1/\alpha\rceil - 1$ then binds. We pre-registered this limitation as an amendment
before running the arm, and the measurement landed where the amendment predicted.
