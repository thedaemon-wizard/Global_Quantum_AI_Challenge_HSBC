\section{Validation plan}
\label{sec:validation}

The plan below is not aspirational: every step was executed in this study on public data, and
the results are in Section~\ref{sec:results}. What a PoC sprint changes is the data, not the
protocol. Some pre-registered items were \emph{not} executed --- two kernel hypotheses and the
entanglement ablation, closed by the screens, and amendment~A7's drift measurements --- and the
appendix enumerates them, because a plan that cannot say which steps ran is not one.

\textbf{Success is a certificate, not a score.} A PoC succeeds if the band-conditional
false-decline rate is certified at a level the business names, with family-wise error control,
and the certificate holds on a block never used to build it. Average precision and ROC AUC are
reported alongside but decide nothing. Failure is equally well defined --- the admissible
set comes back empty, which is what happens here at the tightest band budget --- and the
acceptance test is the held-out read reported in Section~\ref{sec:results}.

\textbf{Size the evaluation block to the power target first.} A detectable-effect ceiling is
fixed \emph{before} the comparison and the block sized to it, which is the one change that would
make a quantum-versus-classical comparison here informative; Section~\ref{sec:quantum} reports
what happens when it is not.

\textbf{Threshold change management.} $\lambda$ moves on every recalibration, so it needs an
owner and a cadence rather than a rerun. Model risk approves each move quarterly, matching the
rolling window the fraud-rate definition already uses, and out of cycle whenever realised in-band
risk crosses the certified $\alpha$. If a recalibration fails to certify, the rule falls back to
the previously certified $\lambda$, and failing that to the incumbent scorer with the band
closed --- degrading to today's behaviour rather than to an uncertified threshold. Phase~II runs
the challenger in shadow first, scoring every authorisation and acting on none.

\textbf{What keeps the plan honest.} Grids, splits and thresholds are hashed before any model is
fitted, the hypotheses are frozen with dated amendments, and every retraction is recorded ---
\ClaimDecisionEntries{} decision entries, including ones that removed results already written
down. Reproduction is \texttt{make smoke \&\& make reproduce \&\& make check}.
